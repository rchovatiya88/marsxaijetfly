name: mars-premium-playtest
description: Plan premium pricing, playtests, mission scope, and distribution for the Mars Jetbike game using its research and roadmap. Use for product validation and commercial planning.
---

Read `docs/RESEARCH.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `docs/PROJECT_MEMORY.md` from the repository root. The explicit business choice is premium paid. The current prototype is not a paid-ready product.

Separate sourced platform facts, proposed experiments and observed player evidence. Verify current platform payment rules and event requirements before acting. itch.io's ordinary HTML5 embed currently takes donations; use the documented downloadable route or a separately designed entitlement service for paid access. Never equate local best-score storage with purchase validation.

Test the flight/combat differentiator before expanding content. Use fresh players, an uncoached first run, a concrete task sequence and voluntary replay. The roadmap's ten-player gates and proposed $9.99/$14.99 prices are internal hypotheses, not external benchmarks. Record sample size, build, hardware, blockers and exact player behavior.

For economics, state price, discount, channel deduction, refunds, costs and conversion assumptions separately. Show sensitivity and break-even; do not infer sales from review counts or promise income. Use real gameplay in marketing drafts, and distinguish an intended store link from an existing destination.

Save findings and next decisions in repository docs. Planning does not authorize purchases, publishing, signing agreements or sending outreach. Keep those actions separate from the local research and build work.
