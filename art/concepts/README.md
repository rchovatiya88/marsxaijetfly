## Design review

The fork, cyan high bridge, sheltered low passage and amber extraction read clearly. Use the scene composition and palette as a target; the image is more detailed than the first graybox budget. The generated oversized Warden and gun-bearing pose do not establish the supplied model's animations or abilities. Keep the actual Warden grounded and validate its silhouette at game scale. Build and test geometry before matching atmospheric polish.
